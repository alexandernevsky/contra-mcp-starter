# Freelancer Profile Positioning & Bio Template

This template defines the high-converting copy structure for your Contra profile.
Update the bracketed fields with your own details.

---

## 1. Professional Title / Headline
*Max 60 characters. Avoid generic titles like "Freelancer" or "Designer". Focus on outcome and duality.*

```text
[Role 1] & [Role 2 / Builder] — [Value Proposition or Niche]
```

**Examples:**
- `AI Product Designer & Builder`
- `Senior Full-Stack Engineer & Cloud Architect`
- `Technical Brand Designer & Framer Partner`
- `Growth Product Strategist & Automation Engineer`

---

## 2. Profile Bio (About Section)
*Max 2,800 characters. Structured for quick scanning by founders and hiring managers.*

```markdown
[One-sentence positioning: who you are, what you build, and what makes your approach different from typical freelancers or agencies.]

### What you can bring to me:
• [Primary project type: e.g. 0-to-1 Web Application Design & Engineering]
• [Secondary project type: e.g. Rapid MVP & Interactive Functional Prototypes in 7–14 days]
• [Operational / systems: e.g. Business Process Automation, APIs, and AI Agent Workflows]
• [High-leverage design: e.g. Design Systems, Production-ready UI, and Brand Codebases]

### How I work:
- **Direct collaboration**: You work directly with a senior builder. Zero account management overhead, zero subcontracting.
- **Fast production cycles**: Rapid, weekly shipped milestones using modern tools and AI-assisted engineering.
- **Full ownership**: All source code, Figma files, tokens, and deployed infrastructure belong 100% to you from day one.

Based in [City, Country]. Available worldwide for select client projects, fixed-scope sprints, and monthly retainers.
```
